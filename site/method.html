Four elements, one loop. What is learned in review feeds the next read of the market.

Interpret context and identify what matters.

  • Context and market structure
  • The few variables that matter now
  • Prioritization of information

In the worked scenarioAn index presses against a multi-week range high, with a policy decision two days out. What matters: rate expectations, breadth, and behaviour at the high. What does not: social-media targets.

Form a clear thesis, evaluate conditions, and define invalidation.

  • Thesis, conditions and alternatives
  • Conviction, stated honestly
  • Invalidation defined in advance

In the worked scenarioThesis: a close above the range, with broad participation, suggests the range is resolving upwards. Invalidation: a close back inside it. Both are written down before entry.

Translate the decision into disciplined action under uncertainty.

  • Rule-based action
  • Risk set before entry
  • A plan for the moment of hesitation

In the worked scenarioPosition size comes from the distance to invalidation and a risk limit set in advance. If hesitation appears, the written plan decides, not the feeling.

Separate decision quality from outcome and improve the process.

  • Trade reviews and execution audits
  • Poor decision ≠ unfavourable outcome
  • Lessons feed the next decision

02 Decision Quality

A decision that can be tested.

Five things, all stated before action.

  1. Thesis

    What should happen, and why?

    Worked exampleA close above the range high, with broad participation, suggests the range is resolving upwards.

  2. Conditions

    What must be true for this to hold?

    Worked exampleBreadth confirms, and the policy decision does not surprise against expectations.

  3. Alternatives

    What else could this be?

    Worked exampleA failed break and a return into the range.

  4. Conviction

    How strong is the case?

    Worked exampleModerate. Event risk caps it, and size reflects that.

  5. Invalidation

    What proves it wrong, and when?

    Worked exampleA daily close back inside the range.

Any element missing? The decision is unfinished, and acting on it is a guess.

03 Execution Under Uncertainty

Action that follows the plan.

Analysis becomes action, and the plan holds.

  • Translate analysis into action

    The decision becomes specific, rule-based steps.

  • Manage risk

    Size and exit set before entry, honoured during it.

  • Handle hesitation

    A response to doubt is agreed in advance.

  • Follow the process

    The defined process overrules impulse.

The plan decides, not the feeling.

04 Review & Refinement

Judge the decision, not just the result.

Poor decision and unfavourable outcome are not the same thing.

  • Trade reviews

    Structured analysis of each decision after the fact.

  • Execution audits

    Did the action match the plan, step by step?

  • Decision vs. outcome

    A sound decision can lose. A weak one can win.

  • Feedback loops

    What is learned feeds back into Market Intelligence.

Applied example

Reasoning before the outcome is known.

An equity index presses a multi-week range high. A policy decision is two days away. Follow the reasoning through all four elements.

Illustrative scenario. Not a trade recommendation or a real trade.

1 · Market Intelligence

The index is testing a multi-week range high. A policy decision is due in two days. The outcome is unknown.

What matters

  • Rate expectations going into the decision
  • How broad the advance is
  • How price behaves at the range high

What does not

  • Social-media price targets
  • Headlines unrelated to the decision
  • Opinions with no stated conditions

Output. A short list of variables and a view of the regime: range-bound, with a possible break.

2 · Decision Quality

The trader considers a long position and writes the decision down before acting.

Thesis
A close above the range, with broad participation, means the range is resolving upwards.
Conditions
Breadth confirms. The policy decision does not surprise.
Alternative
A failed break and a return into the range.
Conviction
Moderate. Event risk caps it.
Invalidation
A daily close back inside the range.

Output. A decision that can be tested and, if wrong, exited without debate.

3 · Execution

The conditions are met, or they are not. Either way the plan answers first.

  • Size comes from the distance to invalidation and a risk limit set in advance.
  • Entry only if the written conditions are met. Otherwise, no trade.
  • If hesitation appears, the plan decides. If price gaps through it, a defined response applies.

Output. Action that matches the plan, whatever the emotion of the moment.
