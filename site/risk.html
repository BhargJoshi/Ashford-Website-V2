01Trading involves risk of loss

Trading and investing in financial instruments carry a high level of risk. You can lose some or all of the capital you commit. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

02No guarantees

Ashford does not guarantee profitability, consistency or any outcome. Past performance, whether yours, ours or anyone else’s, is not a reliable indicator of future results.

03Leverage and derivatives

Leveraged products, including futures and options, can magnify losses as well as gains. Losses can exceed the capital you initially committed. Make sure you understand how a product works before you trade it.

04Market conditions

Prices can move quickly and unpredictably. Volatility, low liquidity, gaps and slippage can cause orders to execute at prices different from those you expected, or not at all.

05Education, not advice

Ashford provides education and mentorship. We do not issue trading signals or tips, and we do not provide personal investment advice. Nothing we publish or teach takes your circumstances, objectives or risk tolerance into account.

06Examples are illustrative

Scenarios, Decision Labs, Trade Autopsies and examples on this website and in our programs are illustrative. They are not real trades, recommendations or predictions, and they do not show typical or expected results.

07Your decisions are your own

You are solely responsible for your trading and investment decisions and their results. Developing a decision process does not remove risk, and a sound decision can still lose money.

08Seek independent advice

Consider whether trading is suitable for you. Where appropriate, consult a licensed financial adviser, and a tax or legal professional, before you act.
