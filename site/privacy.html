01Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Ashford Capital (“Ashford”, “we”) handles personal information collected through this website, including the readiness assessment and the contact form.

02Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Your name, email address and, optionally, phone number.
  • Your assessment responses: market experience, primary difficulty, current process and development objective.
  • Any message or note you write to us.

Our hosting provider may also keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

03How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and suggest an appropriate pathway or next step.
  • To understand who our programs are for, and to improve them.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to send promotions you have not asked for.

04Basis for processing

We process the information you submit on the basis of your consent, given when you submit a form. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing does not affect processing that already took place.

05Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the website and handle enquiries, such as hosting and form-handling providers, and only for those purposes. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

06Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics or other tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

07Retention

We keep personal information for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, or as the law requires, and then delete or anonymize it.

08Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including data protection law in India, you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal information, withdraw consent, or raise a concern about how it is handled. Contact us using the details on the Contact page.

09Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Children

This website and our programs are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is the current one.
