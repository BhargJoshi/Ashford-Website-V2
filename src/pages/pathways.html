01 · Learn to See

Market Readers

Build the ability to interpret.

Purpose: independent market perception.

{{fig:readers}}
  • {{ic:person}}

    Who it is for

    Traders with some market exposure whose knowledge sits as isolated concepts, and who lean on indicators, opinions or content to read the market.

  • {{ic:process}}

    How it works

    Live learning on concept material, applied through guided practice. Assessments check understanding; feedback closes the loop.

  • {{ic:clipboard}}

    Readiness

    Beyond the beginner stage: familiar with core concepts, typically six months of exposure. Confirmed through the readiness assessment.

{{ic:gap:sm}}Problems it addresses

  • Fragmented market understanding
  • Information overload, unclear priorities
  • Dependence on indicators and outside opinions
  • Concepts known, but not when and why to use them

{{ic:plan:sm}}Capabilities it aims to develop

  • Organize existing market knowledge
  • Understand context, prioritize what is relevant
  • Develop structured interpretations and assessments
  • Apply concepts through guided practice and feedback

{{ic:material:sm}}Included

  • Live learning
  • Guided practice
  • Concept material
  • Assessments
  • Feedback

{{ic:chart:sm}}What progress should look like

Structured, independent interpretation. Less reliance on indicators and outside opinions. A clearer sense of what matters in a given market.

02 · Learn to Act

Market Operators

Build the ability to decide and execute.

Purpose: consistent execution under live conditions.

{{fig:operators}}
  • {{ic:person}}

    Who it is for

    Traders who understand markets but struggle to act on that understanding consistently when real money is at stake.

  • {{ic:process}}

    How it works

    Daily live execution in real-time conditions, with risk guidance during the session and structured performance review after it.

  • {{ic:clipboard}}

    Readiness

    Working market knowledge and readiness to practise in live conditions. Confirmed through the readiness assessment.

{{ic:gap:sm}}Problems it addresses

  • Hesitation and constant second-guessing
  • Analysis paralysis and missed entries
  • Emotional instability in live markets
  • Inconsistent risk execution under pressure

{{ic:plan:sm}}Capabilities it aims to develop

  • Translate analysis into actionable decisions
  • Define risk and decision conditions
  • Build execution discipline under uncertainty
  • Review decisions, behaviour and execution quality

{{ic:material:sm}}Included

  • Daily live execution sessions
  • Real-time trading
  • Risk guidance
  • Performance review

{{ic:chart:sm}}What progress should look like

Consistent, process-driven behaviour. Risk defined before entry and followed. A clear view of what was decision, execution and outcome.

03 · Learn to Allocate

Capital Thinkers

Build the ability to allocate and preserve capital.

Purpose: capital preservation and scalable growth. Elite pathway.

{{fig:thinkers}}
  • {{ic:person}}

    Who it is for

    Experienced participants ready to move from individual trades to capital, portfolio and regime-level judgment.

  • {{ic:process}}

    How it works

    Strategic discussions and regime-level intelligence, applied to portfolio systems and examined through periodic capital reviews.

  • {{ic:clipboard}}

    Readiness

    Elite pathway. Established market experience, confirmed through the readiness assessment and a conversation.

{{ic:gap:sm}}Problems it addresses

  • Activity prioritized over longevity
  • Position thinking without portfolio context
  • A limited view of capital cycles and regimes
  • Short-horizon reviews of long-horizon decisions

{{ic:plan:sm}}Capabilities it aims to develop

  • Understand capital cycles and market regimes
  • Develop portfolio and position-level thinking
  • Weigh risk, exposure and allocation
  • Review decisions through a longer-term lens

{{ic:material:sm}}Included

  • Strategic discussions
  • Macro and regime intelligence
  • Portfolio systems
  • Capital reviews

{{ic:chart:sm}}What progress should look like

Deliberate allocation and exposure decisions, reviewed over longer horizons and oriented toward preservation and longevity.
