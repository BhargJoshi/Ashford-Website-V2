Knowledge is not a decision

Knowing what a support level is, what a policy rate does or what a stop-loss is describes the world. A decision commits to one reading of it, with a defined risk, before the outcome is known. These are different skills, and only the first is taught by most education.

Where execution breaks

Under live conditions, three things tend to happen. Hesitation: conviction and doubt compete at the point of entry. Conflicting interpretations: several valid readings, and no rule for choosing between them. Inconsistent execution: a plan that made sense after hours is abandoned once money is at risk.

What Executional Clarity is

Executional Clarity is the ability to move, repeatably, from market context to a defined decision to disciplined action, and to review that chain honestly afterwards. It is built by practising the chain, not by adding inputs to it.

In practice

That means deciding what matters before looking at price, writing the thesis and its invalidation in advance, sizing from the risk rather than from excitement, and judging each trade by its reasoning and execution before its result.
